## Forks

Please fork. That's what MIT is for.

The one thing we ask is that your fork not use the Isopolis name or logo in a way that
suggests it's endorsed by, affiliated with, or came from Axiomic. In practice: give your
fork its own name and its own icon. Someone landing on it should be able to tell at a
glance whose work they're looking at.

## Saying what your project is

Describing your project accurately is always fine. "Based on Isopolis", "a fork of
Isopolis", "originally derived from Isopolis": all true, all welcome, no permission
needed. The line is between describing the origin and wearing the name.
